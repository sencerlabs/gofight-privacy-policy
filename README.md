# GoFight Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy applies to version 1.0.0 of the GoFight Android app.

## Data collection and sharing
GoFight does not send personal information to the developer or third parties. The game works offline and does not include accounts, chat, advertising, in-app purchases, analytics or remote crash reporting. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

## Information stored on your device
Your fighter, opponent, difficulty, graphics quality, control layout, audio preferences, earned career progress, gold, gems, fame, daily goals and equipped collection items are saved in the app's private storage on your device. They are not sent to a server. You can delete this information by clearing the app's data in Android settings. Uninstalling the app also deletes its local data. Android automatic backup is disabled in the release package.

## Permissions
The game may use vibration permission for gameplay feedback. It does not request internet permission.

## Store services
The store you download the app from may process information under its own privacy policy. This does not mean the game collects that information.

## Changes
If the game's data handling changes, this policy and the store's data safety information will be updated.

## Contact
For privacy questions, contact the developer using the support email shown in the Developer contact section of GoFight's Google Play listing.
